# Saudi riyal symbol

`sar.svg` is the official Saudi riyal symbol, copied unchanged on 2026-11-08 from the Saudi
Central Bank (SAMA) guidelines page:
https://www.sama.gov.sa/en-US/Currency/SRS/Pages/Guideline.aspx
(file: https://www.sama.gov.sa/ar-sa/Currency/Documents/Saudi_Riyal_Symbol-2.svg).

Do not redraw or edit it. Follow SAMA's guidelines for its use.
