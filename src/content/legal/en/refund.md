This policy is part of the [Terms and Conditions](/en/terms/). It applies to subscriptions to the paid applications in LiwanOS.

## The rule

If you make the first payment for your subscription to LiwanOS and change your mind, you can get the full amount back as long as you ask within 14 days of that payment. The rule is the same for monthly and annual subscriptions.

## When the 14 days start

They start on the day of the first payment you make to LiwanOS from your account, not on the day you signed up or began the free trial. The free trial lasts 14 days and costs nothing, so there is nothing to refund from it.

## What is not refunded

- Renewals: payments after the first one, monthly or annual, are not refundable. To avoid an upcoming renewal, cancel before its date.
- Fees you paid to other companies directly, such as Meta, TikTok and payment gateways. You pay these to them, they are not part of LiwanOS charges, and we cannot return them.

## Cancelling and refunding

Cancelling stops the next renewal, and your subscription continues until the end of the paid period. A refund returns the amount of your first payment within the period above and ends the paid subscription.

## How to ask for a refund

Send your request from the [contact page](/en/contact/) with your company name, your account email and, if you know it, the date of the first payment. We review it and reply within {{REFUND_REVIEW_PERIOD}}.

## How you get the money back

We return the amount by the same method you paid with, card or bank transfer, within {{REFUND_PAYOUT_PERIOD}} of approving the request. Your payment provider may take longer to show it in your account.

## After a refund

When a refund is made, your paid subscription ends and your company moves to the free core within its limits. Your data stays under the [Terms](/en/terms/) and the [Data Processing Agreement](/en/dpa/).
