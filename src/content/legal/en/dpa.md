This agreement governs the processing of personal data that your company enters into LiwanOS. It is part of the [Terms and Conditions](/en/terms/) and applies to every customer using the platform. The parties are the customer ("controller") and {{LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME}} ("processor"), commercial registration no. {{COMMERCIAL_REGISTRATION_NUMBER}}, registered address: {{REGISTERED_ADDRESS}}.

## Subject and duration

We process data to run the applications the customer subscribed to. Processing lasts as long as the subscription, then continues as set out in the "End of the agreement" section.

## Types of data and data subjects

- Data subjects: the customer's clients and prospects, its staff and account users, and anyone else whose data the customer enters.
- Types of data: name, contact details, message content, tasks and files, and any other data the customer places in the platform.

## Customer instructions

We process data on the customer's documented instructions, which are this agreement and the customer's use of the platform, and not for any other purpose. If we believe an instruction breaks the law, we will tell the customer.

## Customer responsibilities

The customer is responsible for having a legal basis to collect its data and enter it into the platform, for giving data subjects the notices required, and for the accuracy of its instructions.

## Confidentiality

Our staff who can reach the data are bound by confidentiality and have access only as far as their work needs.

## Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the data, including a separate database for each company and limits on access permissions to our systems.

## Hosting and transfers

The platform is hosted in Saudi Arabia, and the customer's data stays there. It leaves only when the customer uses Liwan AI and consents to sending specific data to an external AI model provider, and only to the extent of that consent.

## Sub-processors

We use a hosting provider, and an AI model provider when Liwan AI is turned on with the customer's consent. We bind them to what is needed to protect the data, and we stay responsible to the customer for our obligations. We tell the customer before adding a new sub-processor that handles its data, and the customer may object.

Payment gateways and channels the customer connects itself, such as Meta and TikTok, are the customer's own direct relationships and are not our sub-processors. The customer's money does not pass through Liwan.

## Helping with data subject requests

If a data subject sends us a request about the customer's data, we refer it to the customer. We help the customer, as far as reasonable, to answer legal requests.

## Breach notification

If we learn of a breach affecting the customer's data, we notify the customer within {{BREACH_NOTICE_PERIOD}} of becoming aware, and tell it what we know about its nature, the data involved and what we are doing about it.

## Audit

The customer may ask us for reasonable information showing we comply with this agreement. Any on-site audit is agreed in advance so that it does not disrupt the service or expose other customers' data.

## End of the agreement

When the subscription ends we let the customer download its data for {{RETENTION_PERIOD}}, then delete it from our systems, except what the law requires us to keep. The customer may ask in writing for faster deletion.
