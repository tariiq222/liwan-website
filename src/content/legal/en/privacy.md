This policy explains what data we collect, why, who we share it with, and how to reach us about it. The responsible company is {{LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME}} (commercial registration no. {{COMMERCIAL_REGISTRATION_NUMBER}}, registered address: {{REGISTERED_ADDRESS}}), called "LiwanOS" below.

## Our website

This website stores no personal data about you, and we use no third-party service to collect forms. The only form is the contact form, and it sends what you type to the LiwanOS platform directly. It asks for your company email.

What happens when you open the website:

- It loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address.
- The contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check that you are a person, so Cloudflare receives your IP address.
- Your light or dark theme choice is kept only in your own browser and never reaches us.
- If you open the sign-up link from an ad campaign, we pass the campaign details on to the sign-up link only, and this website does not keep them.

## What we collect when you use the platform

- Account data: your name, company email, company name and permissions.
- Your company's data: what you and your team enter into the applications, such as customer records, messages, tasks and files.
- Subscription data: your plan and payment dates. {{CONFIRM_CARD_DATA: card details are handled by the payment provider and we do not keep your card number}}.
- Operational data: technical logs needed to run and protect the service and to fix faults.

## Why we use it, and on what basis

- To run the platform and provide the applications you subscribed to. Basis: performing our contract with you.
- To manage your account, subscription and payments, and to answer your requests. Basis: the contract, and our legal obligations.
- To protect the service and prevent abuse. Basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.
- To send important service notices. Basis: the contract.
- Where we rely on your consent, such as sending data to the AI model provider, you can withdraw it at any time. Withdrawing does not affect what happened before.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your company's data for advertising.

## Your customers' data

The customer data you enter in the platform remains your responsibility as the controller. We process it on your behalf under the [Data Processing Agreement](/en/dpa/). You need a legal basis to collect and use it.

## Liwan AI

When you use Liwan AI, data you have consented to may be sent to an external AI model provider so it can process it and return an answer. The provider may be outside the Kingdom. The basis for this transfer is your explicit consent, and we do not send data for this purpose without it. Read the consent before you turn it on, and choose what you allow.

## Where data is kept

The platform is hosted in Saudi Arabia. Each company has its own separate database. Data leaves it in two cases only: what Liwan AI sends, with your consent, to the external model provider, and what you send yourself through the channels and payment gateways you connect.

## Who we share data with

- The hosting provider we run the platform on.
- Payment gateways: each customer connects its own gateway account. What your customers pay you moves directly between you and your gateway, and LiwanOS does not receive it.
- Channels you connect yourself, such as Meta and TikTok: once connected, messages move between you and that platform under its terms, and you pay its fees to it directly.
- The external AI model provider, as described above and with your consent.
- Public authorities, when the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while your subscription lasts. After an account ends we return your data on request in a common format, keep it for {{RETENTION_PERIOD}}, then delete it, except what the law requires us to keep.

You can ask to see your personal data, get a copy of it, correct it, delete it, or object to how it is processed, as far as the law allows. Send your request through: {{PRIVACY_REQUEST_CHANNEL}}. We verify who you are first, then reply within a reasonable time. If your request concerns the customer data of a company using the platform, that company is the controller, and we may refer you to it.

If you believe we have not handled your data as the law requires, you can complain to the competent authority in the Kingdom: {{COMPETENT_AUTHORITY}}.

## Protecting data

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data from unauthorized access or loss, including a separate database for each company and limits on who can reach our systems. No protection is absolute, and we will tell you if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

## Updates to this policy

We may update this policy and show the date of the last update at the top. We tell you about material changes inside the platform or at your account email.
