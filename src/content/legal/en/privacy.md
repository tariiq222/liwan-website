This policy explains what data we collect, why, who we share it with, and how to reach us about it. The responsible company is {{LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME}} (commercial registration no. {{COMMERCIAL_REGISTRATION_NUMBER}}, registered address: {{REGISTERED_ADDRESS}}).

## Our website

This website stores no personal data about you, and we use no third-party service to collect forms. The only form is the contact form, and it sends what you type to the LiwanOS platform directly. It asks for your company email. If you open the sign-up link from an ad campaign, we may pass the campaign details on to the sign-up link only, and we do not keep them.

## What we collect when you use the platform

- Account data: your name, company email, company name and permissions.
- Your company's data: what you and your team enter into the applications, such as customer records, messages, tasks and files.
- Subscription data: your plan and payment dates. Card details are handled by the payment provider and we do not keep your card number.
- Operational data: technical logs needed to run and protect the service and to fix faults.

## Why we use it

- To run the platform and provide the applications you subscribed to.
- To manage your account, subscription and payments, and to answer your requests.
- To protect the service and prevent abuse.
- To send important service notices.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your company's data for advertising.

## Your customers' data

The customer data you enter in the platform remains your responsibility as the party that decides how it is used. We process it on your behalf under the [Data Processing Agreement](/en/dpa/). You need a legal basis to collect and use it.

## Liwan AI

When you use Liwan AI, data you have consented to may be sent to an external AI model provider so it can process it and return an answer. We do not send data for this purpose without your consent. Read the consent before you turn it on, and choose what you allow.

## Where data is kept

The platform is hosted in Saudi Arabia. Each company has its own separate database. The only exception is what Liwan AI sends, with your consent, to the external model provider as described above.

## Who we share data with

- The hosting provider we run the platform on.
- Payment gateways: each customer connects its own gateway account, and Liwan does not receive your customers' money. Payments move directly between you and your gateway.
- Channels you connect yourself, such as Meta and TikTok: once connected, messages move between you and that platform under its terms, and you pay its fees to it directly.
- The external AI model provider, as described above and with your consent.
- Public authorities, when the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while your subscription lasts. After an account ends we keep it for {{RETENTION_PERIOD}} so you can download it, then delete it, except what the law requires us to keep.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to how it is processed, as far as the law allows. Send your request through: {{PRIVACY_REQUEST_CHANNEL}}. We verify who you are first, then reply within a reasonable time. If your request concerns the customer data of a company using the platform, that company decides how it is handled, and we may refer you to it.

## Protecting data

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data from unauthorized access or loss, including a separate database for each company and limits on who can reach our systems. No protection is absolute, and we will tell you if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

## Updates to this policy

We may update this policy and show the date of the last update at the top. We tell you about material changes inside the platform or at your account email.
